# Profile

Who Talos is talking to. Loaded into every prompt, unchanged and unsearched —
these are the facts that should always be there, not ones to go looking for.

Keep it short. Every line here is in the context window of every single reply,
competing with the conversation for room. A page of biography makes a worse
assistant, not a better-informed one: facts that are needed rarely belong in a
skill, and facts that are needed once belong in the conversation.

Edit freely. It is read fresh on every reply, so changes take effect without
restarting.
